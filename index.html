Hoppadooo

Gentle learning cartoons for toddlers (ages 2–4): colors, counting, shapes, letters and more, with Bobo the bunny and Pip the chick.

Children answer questions, say the words out loud and move along, so they learn by joining in. New videos every day. Every character, song and voice is original.
