Privacy Policy

Hoppadooo (YouTube: @Hoppadooo) and its publishing tool Kids Autopilot. Last updated: 29 October 2026.

What Kids Autopilot is

Kids Autopilot is a private tool used only by the owner of the Hoppadooo YouTube channel. It uploads the channel's own original videos to that channel and reads that channel's own statistics (views, watch time) to decide good publishing times. It is not offered to the public, and no one else signs in to it.

Google / YouTube data it uses

  • Upload and manage videos (YouTube Data API: youtube.upload, youtube) – only on the channel owner's own channel.
  • Read channel analytics (YouTube Analytics API: yt-analytics.readonly) – aggregated statistics of the owner's own channel only.

The access token is stored encrypted in the owner's private GitHub repository settings and is used only for the actions above. The tool's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, store, sell or share personal information about viewers.
  • We do not access any other person's Google account or YouTube channel.
  • We do not use data for advertising, profiling or training AI models.

Children's privacy

Hoppadooo videos are made for children and are published with YouTube's "made for kids" setting, which turns off comments and personalised ads. We do not knowingly collect any information from children. Viewing happens on YouTube under YouTube's privacy policy and Google's privacy policy.

Removing access

The channel owner can revoke the tool's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
